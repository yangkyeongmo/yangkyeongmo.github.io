Why is it so hard to write an article?

Let's say you have only 24 hours to write a blog post.

What's difference between an expert and normal person? Procedure.

Natural language models could drastically reduce time to write. Bottleneck of human is its output speed. Our output speed is very limited: 200 wpm for stenographer. Currently known max wpm is about 300 wpm, while LLM could output whooping 2000 wpm![^1]

If we use a properly supposed structure, we could 1) reduce cognitive burden 2) maintain overall context 3) ignore very limit originated by being human.

My suggestion of writing an article with LLM is analogous to dropping a drip of ink in a cell of water. Our traditional method is like using a needle to draw a picture. This new method is like water paint. We formulate a color palette beforehand, then brush it into a canvas. Then water is spread out, effortlessly filling in as what we wanted. If we need a more fine-grained drawing, then we change our tool, but not our palette.

Suppose we want to write about something. What do you want to write about? Let's say I want to write about christmas.

You have something in your mind. You've got some grasp of what you could do.. just in your mind. Nothing has been actually made.

(The note had a diagram here.)

Naive approach is to write sentence by sentence and be inspired by it when writing the next sentence. According to previous data, however, we need 15 minutes to write a whole 3000 word essay. This doesn't seem too long unless you already know exactly what you want to write. Not only we need a full 15 minutes, it also takes our precious energy that we could've put into thinking instead of typing.

(The note had a diagram here.)

Wait, but aren't there people who write as their profession? There must be best practice to avoid this kind of inefficiency, right? There is one approach I know, which is writing an article iteratively. Incremental vs Iterative.

When we try to write something, there must be some themes, assertions, reasons, conclusions and all other sorts of concepts that could fall into some logics. Like all other concepts those would convey a structure. If we understand about a structure, then filling the gap is easy. Iteratively, we could treat the structures as logical items, then the items would also convey a structure, and so on and so on.. then we would finally be at the very beginning. We start from there, draw whole picture iteratively, while keeping our eye on the finish line.

Writing is lossy compression about what we already have in our mind. LLM's one of the best feature is summarization, which is lossy compression.
